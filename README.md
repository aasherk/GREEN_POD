# 🌱 GREENPOD
## Solar-Powered Autonomous Precision Planting Rover

GREENPOD is a solar-assisted agricultural rover designed to make planting more precise, efficient, and environmentally friendly. It combines soil monitoring, automated drilling, biodegradable plant-capsule placement, and soil covering in one robotic system.

## 🌿 Project Overview

Agriculture faces challenges such as labour shortages, inconsistent planting, changing soil conditions, and plastic waste. GREENPOD aims to address these issues by using sensors and automation to help identify suitable planting locations and place plants consistently.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Soil monitoring:** Sensors can measure soil moisture, temperature, pH, and electrical conductivity.
- **Automated drilling:** A drilling mechanism creates a hole at a controlled depth.
- **Biodegradable capsules:** Capsules made from suitable plant- or agricultural-waste materials can hold a pre-germinated plant or bud with organic growing material.
- **Automated placement and covering:** The rover places the capsule in the hole and covers it with soil.
- **Solar-assisted power:** A rechargeable battery supported by solar energy helps reduce reliance on external charging.
- **Planting records:** The system can be extended to record planting locations and associated soil readings.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. The rover moves to a potential planting location.
2. Soil sensors collect readings such as moisture, temperature, pH, and electrical conductivity.
3. The control system evaluates the readings to help identify a potentially suitable location.
4. The drilling mechanism creates a hole at the selected depth.
5. The capsule delivery mechanism releases a GREENPOD capsule into the hole.
6. The rover covers the capsule with soil.
7. Soil readings and planting information can be saved for later review, if data logging is implemented.

## 🧩 Main System Modules

- **Mobility module:** Chassis, wheels, and drive motors for rover movement.
- **Soil-sensing module:** Sensors for collecting soil-condition data.
- **Drilling module:** Drill, motor, and mounting mechanism.
- **Capsule delivery module:** A holder and release mechanism for biodegradable capsules.
- **Power module:** Rechargeable battery and solar charging support.
- **Control module:** Microcontroller and motor-control electronics.
- **Optional data module:** Storage or wireless communication for planting records.

> The final component list depends on the prototype design and the hardware selected.

## 🌍 Sustainability Goals

GREENPOD is intended to:
- Reduce repetitive manual planting work.
- Improve consistency in plant placement.
- Encourage the reuse of agricultural and plant waste.
- Reduce dependence on conventional plastic planting materials.
- Support more informed planting decisions through soil data.
- Explore renewable-energy support for agricultural robotics.

## 🚧 Development Status

GREENPOD is a project concept/prototype under development. Sensor accuracy, autonomous navigation, soil suitability decisions, capsule biodegradability, drilling performance, and field reliability must be tested before practical deployment. Soil sensors provide measurements or estimates; they do not automatically provide a complete or definitive assessment of all soil nutrients.

## 🔮 Future Improvements

- GPS or other autonomous navigation.
- Improved soil-analysis and planting-location algorithms.
- Real-time data display and remote monitoring.
- GPS-tagged planting records.
- Adjustable drilling depth and capsule release.
- Field testing across different soil types and crops.
- Testing capsule strength, plant survival, and biodegradation.
